# Security and Confidentiality

## Public-safe content rule

Do not add credentials, recovery codes, MFA details, personal information, customer names, employee names, phone numbers, email addresses, ticket links, internal URLs, production IP addresses, hostnames, store or site identifiers, serial numbers, screenshots from private systems, or proprietary procedures.

## Example data

Use reserved documentation domains such as `example.invalid` and documentation-only address ranges when an example is necessary. Never reuse a real production value.

## Operational boundary

These documents are examples of support reasoning. Follow the organization’s current policy, access model, change process, vendor guidance, and maintenance window. Stop and escalate when the required authority, recovery path, or impact assessment is missing.

## Reporting a concern

Do not post sensitive evidence in a public issue. Describe the concern at a high level and move the discussion to a private channel before sharing details.
